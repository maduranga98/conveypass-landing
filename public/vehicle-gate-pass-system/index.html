What is a vehicle gate pass system?

A vehicle gate pass system decides which vehicles and drivers are allowed through your gate. Approved vehicles get a pass, the gate checks that pass and the driver's documents before entry, and every movement is recorded.

On paper, that means a register, a stack of photocopied licences and a guard who has to spot an expired date by eye. ConvoyPass turns it into one digital record: the pass, the documents, the penalties and the visit history all live together, and the eligibility check runs automatically every time a vehicle arrives.

How the gate pass system works

Six steps, from registering a vehicle to renewing its pass next month.

  1. Register vehicles and drivers

    Add each vehicle and driver once, with licence, insurance and vehicle licence expiry dates.

  2. Inspect and approve

    A Safety Officer inspects the vehicle. One inspection clears multiple trips until something is flagged.

  3. Issue a monthly gate pass

    Eligible vehicles get a QR gate pass for the month. Ineligible ones simply don't.

  4. Scan at the barrier

    The guard scans the pass on any phone and gets a pass or fail in seconds, with the reason.

  5. Track the visit

    Check-in, Yard Officer unloading check and check-out are logged with times and staff names.

  6. Renew at month end

    Review who still qualifies and confirm renewals by hand, or let qualified vehicles renew automatically.

At the barrier

Five checks behind every scan

A paper gate pass only proves someone once said yes. A ConvoyPass scan checks whether the answer is still yes today.

  • Gate pass is active for the current month
  • Driver licence, vehicle insurance and vehicle licence are in date
  • No active penalty or suspension on the driver or vehicle
  • Inspection clearance is still valid
  • Driver isn't already checked in on another visit

Vehicle gate pass system FAQ

What is a vehicle gate pass system?

A vehicle gate pass system controls which vehicles and drivers may enter a site. Each approved vehicle gets a pass, the gate checks that pass and the driver's documents before entry, and every entry and exit is logged. Digital systems such as ConvoyPass run these checks automatically instead of relying on a paper register.

How does a vehicle gate pass work at the gate?

The guard scans the vehicle's QR gate pass with a phone camera. ConvoyPass then checks the pass is valid for the current month, the driver licence and vehicle insurance have not expired, no penalty or suspension is active, and the driver is not already on another visit. The guard sees a clear pass or fail result and the entry is logged.

Do drivers need an app or email account?

No. Drivers carry a QR gate pass and guards and yard staff sign in with a PIN, so nobody at the gate needs an app download or an email address.

What happens when a gate pass expires?

ConvoyPass issues gate passes monthly. An expired pass, or a pass whose driver licence or vehicle insurance has lapsed, fails the gate check and entry is blocked. Admins get advance warnings before documents expire, and the month-end review renews passes for qualified vehicles and drivers, either manually or automatically.

Can a vehicle gate pass system handle contractor and 3PL fleets?

Yes. ConvoyPass is built for sites that let external transport contractors in every day. Each contractor vehicle and driver is registered once, one inspection can clear multiple trips, and penalties can target the driver, the vehicle or both.
