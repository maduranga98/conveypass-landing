How Much Does Gate Pass Management Software Cost? A Buyer's Guide

Pricing12 Nov 20266 min read

When businesses consider replacing paper-based gate registers with digital gate management software, one of the first questions is simple: how much will it cost?

The answer depends on several factors, including the number of vehicles, operating locations, required features and the pricing model offered by the software provider.

Some platforms charge per user, while others offer subscriptions based on vehicle capacity or site requirements.

For manufacturing plants, warehouses and logistics companies, understanding these differences can help identify a solution that fits their operations without paying for unnecessary features. In this guide, we explore common gate pass software pricing models and the subscription packages available with ConvoyPass.

What determines the cost of gate pass management software?

Before comparing software prices, it is important to understand what influences the total subscription cost.

1. Number of registered vehicles

Some platforms calculate their subscription based on the number of vehicles registered in the system. A small warehouse managing 10 contractor vehicles may need a different package from a manufacturing plant managing 80 supplier and contractor vehicles.

2. Number of operating sites

Businesses operating multiple warehouses, factories or distribution centres may need software that supports multiple locations. Check whether the subscription covers one site or allows access management across several facilities.

3. Number of users

Some software providers charge additional fees for administrators, security guards and other employees. This can make costs unpredictable when businesses operate multiple shifts or have frequent staff changes.

4. Available features

The functionality included in each subscription also matters. Consider whether the software provides:

  • QR code and PIN-based gate passes.
  • Vehicle and driver registration.
  • Driver licence and insurance expiry blocking.
  • Monthly gate pass renewals.
  • Penalty and suspension management.
  • Safety Officer inspections.
  • Yard Officer unloading checks.
  • Custom branding.
  • Priority support.

A lower-priced package may cover basic gate operations, while more advanced packages provide additional compliance and operational capabilities.

Are there hidden costs in gate pass management software?

Before purchasing any software, check the complete subscription terms. Some providers may charge separately for:

  • Initial setup and implementation.
  • Data migration.
  • Additional integrations.
  • Advanced reporting.
  • Training and onboarding.
  • Premium support.
  • Additional sites or vehicle capacity.

Also confirm the billing period, cancellation terms and applicable taxes. With ConvoyPass, the three published packages define their monthly prices, vehicle capacities, site limits and included features.

Why are per-user fees important?

Imagine a manufacturing facility with three security shifts, several administrators and multiple operational officers. A per-user pricing model could increase the subscription cost whenever another employee needs access.

ConvoyPass pricing is based on vehicle capacity and site requirements rather than the number of employees using the platform. Guard and driver turnover does not increase the subscription simply because the number of users changes.

What should you look for beyond pricing?

Price should not be the only consideration. Before selecting a platform, ask:

  1. Can it verify driver licence and vehicle insurance validity?
  2. Can security guards scan QR codes using ordinary smartphones?
  3. Does it support PIN-based access?
  4. Can expired documents block vehicle entry?
  5. Can supervisors manage penalties and suspensions?
  6. Does it support vehicle inspections and unloading verification?
  7. Can it manage multiple sites?
  8. Are there additional fees for security guards and administrators?

A suitable system should simplify daily gate operations while giving management better visibility over external vehicle access.
