QR gate passes explained: how they work and why drivers like them

Product27 Oct 20264 min read

A QR gate pass is a unique code tied to one booked visit. Scanning it tells the gate everything it needs to know in a second.

What happens at the barrier

  • The officer scans the pass from the driver's phone or printout.
  • The system confirms the visit is booked, the documents are current and the vehicle matches.
  • The result is clear: allow, or block with the reason shown.

Why drivers prefer it

No app to install, no waiting while someone phones the office, and no repeat paperwork on every visit.

Why sites prefer it

Every scan creates a timestamped entry automatically, so the log writes itself and cannot be backfilled.

Security notes

Passes are single-visit and expire. A screenshot from last week will not get anyone in.
