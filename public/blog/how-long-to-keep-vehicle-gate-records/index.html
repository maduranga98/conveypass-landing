How Long Should You Keep Gate Logs, Driver Licence and Insurance Records?

Records24 Nov 20264 min read

There is usually no single law that tells a site how long to keep gate logs or copies of a contractor driver's licence and insurance. Instead, a few different rules point to a sensible period, and you have to justify the one you choose.

This guide covers the records an industrial site typically holds about visiting vehicles and drivers, the rules that shape how long to keep them, and an example retention schedule you can adapt. It is general information, not legal advice. Check with your legal or compliance adviser before adopting a policy.

Which records are we talking about?

  • Gate entry and exit logs: who entered, in which vehicle, when, and when they left.
  • Driver documents: copies or details of driver licences.
  • Vehicle documents: insurance certificates and vehicle licence or registration details.
  • Inspection, incident and penalty records: safety checks, violations and suspensions, including photos.

Most of these contain personal data about drivers, which is why "keep everything forever" is not a safe default.

UK: data protection sets the limit, other laws set the floor

The UK GDPR does not give fixed retention periods. The ICO says it does not set specific time limits for different types of data; the period depends on how long you need the data for your purposes. It also notes that if another law requires you to keep records for a set period, you should do so.

Two other UK rules are commonly used to justify a period:

  • Personal injury claims. Under section 11 of the Limitation Act 1980, most personal injury claims must be brought within three years of the injury, or three years from the date the claimant knew about it if that is later. Gate and incident records can be evidence of who was on site, so many sites keep them at least this long, and longer for records linked to an actual incident.
  • Accounting records. Companies must keep accounting records for 6 years from the end of the last company financial year they relate to. This applies to gate records only where they support a financial transaction, such as a delivery note matched to an invoice.

US: carrier rules apply to the carrier, not the receiving site

In the US, motor carriers must keep a driver qualification file for each driver they employ, and federal rules require that file to be kept while the driver is employed and for three years after. That obligation sits with the trucking company employing the driver, not with the warehouse or plant the driver visits.

A receiving site still benefits from aligning with it. If your site keeps contractor driver records for the period of the relationship plus three years, your records line up with what the carrier should hold if a dispute arises. Workplace injury and illness logs and state rules can add further requirements, so confirm with local counsel.

Keep the evidence, not just the document

For gate records, what matters later is rarely the document itself. It is proof that the document was valid on the day the vehicle entered. A useful record shows:

  • The expiry date held for the licence or insurance at the time of entry.
  • That the check was performed, and by whom or by which system.
  • Any refusal or suspension, with the reason.

A digital gate pass system captures this automatically on each scan, which is easier to defend than a ticked column in a paper register. See keeping entry and exit records for audits.

Make retention a routine

  1. Write down your retention periods and the reason for each.
  2. Assign one person to review records on a schedule, such as yearly.
  3. Delete or anonymise records that have passed their period, unless an incident or claim is open.
  4. Tell contractors in your site rules what you collect and how long you keep it.

Frequently asked questions

How long should I keep vehicle gate logs?

There is usually no fixed legal period. Many UK sites keep routine gate logs for about three years, matching the usual personal injury claim window, and keep records linked to an incident until the matter is closed. Confirm with your legal adviser.

Does UK GDPR say how long to keep driver documents?

No. UK GDPR does not set fixed periods. You must keep personal data only as long as you need it and be able to justify the period, while following any other law that requires a set retention period.

How long should a business keep copies of a contractor's insurance documents?

A common approach is to keep insurance details for the length of the contractor relationship plus three years, so you can show the vehicle was insured on the dates it entered your site.

Do US warehouses have to keep driver qualification files?

Driver qualification files are the motor carrier's responsibility under federal rules. A receiving site is not usually the carrier, but may still keep its own records of the checks it performed at the gate.
