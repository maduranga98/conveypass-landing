Driver licence and insurance checks: a 5-minute gate routine

Operations20 Oct 20264 min read

Document checks only work if they are fast enough that security staff actually do them every time. Here is a routine that takes seconds per vehicle.

Check before arrival, not at the barrier

Collect licence and insurance details when the visit is booked. By the time the truck arrives, the decision is already made and the gate officer only confirms identity.

The three things to verify

  • The driver holds a licence valid for the vehicle class, with an expiry beyond the visit date.
  • The vehicle has active insurance covering the visit window.
  • The gate pass matches the driver and plate presenting at the barrier.

Handle exceptions consistently

Decide in advance who can override a block, and record why. Consistent rules prevent the quiet exceptions that undermine every other control.

Track expiry dates

Alert contractors 30 days before documents lapse. Most blocks at the gate are avoidable renewals nobody noticed.
