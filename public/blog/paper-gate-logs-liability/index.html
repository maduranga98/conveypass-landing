Why your paper gate log is a liability, not a record

Compliance6 Oct 20265 min read

When something goes wrong on site, the gate log is the first document anyone asks for. Too often it is a clipboard with missing rows and illegible handwriting.

What goes wrong with paper

  • Gaps. Busy shift changes mean entries are skipped or backfilled from memory.
  • No validation. Nobody checks a licence or insurance certificate against the entry, so expired documents walk straight in.
  • No search. Finding every visit by one contractor over six months means paging through binders.

What an auditor or insurer wants

A timestamped, tamper-evident record that shows who entered, in which vehicle, under which valid documents, and who approved them. A paper log cannot prove any of that after the fact.

A better baseline

Move to a digital record where every gate pass is tied to a driver, a vehicle and current documents. Expired items block entry automatically, and the history is searchable in seconds.

Where to start

Run your current paper log against last month's incidents and near misses. Count how many entries you could actually verify. That number is your baseline.
