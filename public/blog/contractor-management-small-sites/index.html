Contractor management for small industrial sites without a full-time safety team

Guides3 Nov 20266 min read

Small and mid-size sites carry the same liability as large ones but rarely have a dedicated safety team. Keep the process light and the record strong.

1. Keep one contractor register

One list of approved companies, their drivers and their vehicles. Everything else refers back to it.

2. Tie access to valid documents

Passes should expire with the documents behind them. If the insurance lapses on the 14th, the pass stops working on the 14th.

3. Use penalties and suspensions

Record breaches, such as speeding on site or skipping sign-in, and apply a clear escalation: warning, penalty, suspension. A visible policy changes behaviour.

4. Review monthly

Look at visit counts, blocked entries and repeat offenders. Fifteen minutes a month is enough to catch problems early.

Pricing should match your size

Look for flat per-site pricing instead of per-user fees, so adding gate staff never raises the bill.
