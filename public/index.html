Frequently asked questions

How much does ConvoyPass cost?

Two plans: a flat rate of $79 per site per month regardless of fleet size, or a $39 per month base plus $1.50 per registered vehicle. There are no per-user fees.

Do drivers and guards need to install an app?

No. Gate access is PIN-based and gate passes are scanned with the camera on any guard's phone, so no proprietary scanners or driver apps are required.

What does ConvoyPass check at the gate?

Driver licence validity, vehicle insurance and gate pass eligibility are verified automatically at check-in. Expired documents block entry, and flagged issues can suspend a driver or vehicle with photo evidence attached for supervisor review.

Is ConvoyPass in use on a real site?

Yes. ConvoyPass is live at an industrial manufacturing site today, handling real vehicle and driver compliance.

What is a vehicle gate pass management system?

It is software that replaces paper gate logs. Each vehicle and driver is registered once, eligibility is checked automatically at the gate, and every entry and exit is recorded in a searchable log. ConvoyPass does this with QR gate passes scanned on a guard's phone.

How do you stop drivers with expired licences or insurance at the gate?

ConvoyPass tracks driver licence and vehicle insurance expiry dates and checks them at check-in. If a document has expired, entry is blocked automatically instead of relying on a guard to spot it on paper.

How is ConvoyPass different from a paper gate register?

A paper register records what a guard writes down. ConvoyPass verifies documents and pass eligibility before entry, attaches photo evidence to flagged issues, and keeps an audit-ready log, so audits do not mean searching through binders.

Who can use ConvoyPass, and how do they sign in?

ConvoyPass has seven roles with permissions enforced at the system level. Guards and yard staff sign in with a PIN, with no app or email account needed. Office-based administrative users can sign in with email.

Can ConvoyPass handle monthly gate passes for contractor fleets?

Yes. A month-end review flags which vehicles and drivers still qualify, and you can confirm renewals manually or set them to renew automatically.
